## The excuse

> I didn't eat your homework. I ate the part where you spelled Biscuit wrong. Twice. The rest is under the couch.

Biscuit denies everything, then confesses to the exact part that insulted him — and
only that part. The rest of the homework is safe. Under the couch. Where it belongs.

## How it works

`main.ts` copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`),
where the sealed scorer checks it is 1–200 characters of UTF-8 text.

```sh
node main.ts
```
